GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before your pickup.

How much will my pickup cost?

Our minimum pickup charge is $75. Pricing depends on load size, weight, access, location and disposal requirements. Send photos and a description so we can discuss a quote. Larger cleanouts and unusually heavy items need an individual assessment.

Can I book same-day pickup?

Call or text to ask about availability. Same-day service is not guaranteed. Our hours are Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–6 PM Eastern; we are closed Sunday.

Do I need to carry everything outside?

Tell us where the items are when requesting your quote. Include stairs, elevators, tight doorways or long carries so we can confirm access and the loading plan.

How should I prepare?

Separate the items that stay from those being removed, keep access paths clear and secure pets. Tell us about gate codes or parking restrictions when confirming your pickup. Ask us before moving heavy items yourself.

What can’t you take?

We don’t take paint, chemicals, oil, fuel, pressurized containers, asbestos, medical waste, ammunition, fireworks or explosives. Appliances, electronics, batteries, tires, yard waste and construction debris require confirmation first.

Will you donate or recycle my items?

If donation or recycling is important to you, mention it when requesting your quote. We’ll discuss options before pickup; acceptance by a particular facility or charity is not guaranteed.

Does sending a quote request book my pickup?

No. Your request starts the conversation. We’ll confirm the items, location, pricing and available pickup time with you before the job is booked.

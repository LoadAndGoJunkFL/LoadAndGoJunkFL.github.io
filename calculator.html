How the calculation works

Base cost = disposal + miles × vehicle rate + your hours × your pay + helper + overhead + other costs + uncertainty allowance.

Quote = the larger of your minimum charge or base cost ÷ (1 − target profit margin). The result rounds up to the next $5.

Your labor is already included as a cost. Profit is what remains after those entered costs. Actual profit changes if actual costs differ.

Before you quote: get photos of all items, confirm stairs and parking, estimate weight and labor, and check your disposal facility’s current commercial fees.
